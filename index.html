🧩 Nonogram

Pick a puzzle, fill it in, and ask for a hint when you're stuck — it'll explain the reasoning, not just the answer.

Photo → puzzle generation, the shared library, and scanning an existing puzzle are on the roadmap — see README.md.

Use the Help menu above to get a hint or check your work — the explanation shows up here.

How to play

Each row and column has a clue: a list of numbers giving the length of every run of filled cells in that line, in order. Fill in cells so every row and column matches its clue, and the picture reveals itself.

Marking cells

  • Pick Fill or ✕ Mark empty in the toolbar, then click/tap a cell to apply it
  • Click a cell already in that state to clear it back to blank
  • Click-and-drag paints the same mark across cells
  • A clue number grays out once its line is satisfied
  • Finishing a line's fills auto-marks its remaining cells with ✕

Getting help

  • Get a hint finds one forced move and explains the reasoning in the panel at the bottom of the screen
  • If no forced move is available, you can dig deeper for a slower, contradiction-based deduction
  • Auto-check flags a mistake the moment you make it; without it, use Check my work any time
  • Remove bad marks clears every cell that doesn't match the solution
  • Clear all resets the puzzle and move history — it can't be undone
